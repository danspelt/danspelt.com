# Mock Client Dress Rehearsal

**Client:** Stephen Kenning, operating as a fictional property management firm  
**Role:** Owner/Manager of a 12-person firm managing 25 buildings  
**Current tools:** Email, spreadsheets, paper notices, a shared WhatsApp group  
**Pain points:** Maintenance requests get lost, residents complain about slow communication, staff spend too much time answering repetitive questions, documents are scattered.

**Your role:** Dan Spelt, full-stack developer, replying from his real business email with real letterhead, real quote, real contract, real guarantee, and real invoice process.

---

## Rehearsal Goals

1. Send the first outreach email using the approved outreach template.
2. Handle a skeptical reply (price too high, not sure if we need this, what's the difference from off-the-shelf software).
3. Discuss billing and payment terms clearly.
4. Negotiate scope without giving away the farm.
5. Send a formal quote, engagement letter, guarantee, and invoice for deposit.
6. Get agreement on what will be built and when.

### Email 3 — Dan's Response (discovery + differentiation + pricing range)

**Subject:** Re: Reducing the back-and-forth for maintenance requests and resident updates

Hi Stephen,

Great question. Buildium and AppFolio are built to serve thousands of different firms, so you end up paying for features you do not use and adapting your workflow to their system. They are also priced for scale — often $150–$500 per month or more, before add-ons.

What I build is tailored to the way your firm actually works. For a 12-person shop managing 25 buildings, that usually means a focused system with only the pieces you need:

- Residents submit maintenance requests through a simple form
- Managers see, assign, and track those requests in one dashboard
- Announcements go out to the right buildings automatically
- Documents are stored and permissioned by role
- Residents can check status without calling or emailing

That kind of focused build typically runs between $4,000 and $7,000 as a one-time cost, plus optional support. Because it is built around your process, you are not locked into a big monthly platform fee forever.

I would still want to hear about your specific workflow before quoting anything. Are you open to a 20-minute discovery call so I can put together a proper quote?

Best,
Dan

### Email 5 — Dan's Response (scope negotiation)

**Subject:** Re: Reducing the back-and-forth for maintenance requests and resident updates

Hi Stephen,

Yes, we can absolutely start smaller. The best way to keep the initial cost down is to build one core workflow first and add the rest later.

For example, we could begin with just the maintenance request flow:

- Resident request form
- Manager dashboard to receive, assign, and mark complete
- Basic status updates residents can see
- Email notifications at key steps

That would be a smaller custom tool, typically in the $2,000–$3,500 range, and you could validate whether it actually saves time before expanding into announcements, documents, and resident logins.

Once the first piece is working, each additional module is quoted separately, so you are never paying for features you do not use.

Would that smaller scope make sense as a starting point?

Best,
Dan
