## Satisfaction Guarantee

I want you to feel confident that the work delivered will actually improve your business.

- **Clear scope, agreed up front.** The project starts with a written summary of what will be built, what it will cost, and when it will be delivered.
- **Review points built in.** You will see progress at key milestones and have a chance to give feedback before the final handoff.
- **A working acceptance period.** After delivery, you have 14 days to test the system and raise any issues covered by the agreed scope. I will fix them.
- **If we cannot deliver what was agreed, we will make it right.** That may mean additional revisions, clarification of requirements, or, in a genuine dispute, a proportional refund of the unresolved portion.

This guarantee covers the work described in the approved quote and does not cover scope changes, third-party service failures, or issues caused by misuse after handoff.

## Continuity Promise: I Will Not Leave Your Software Behind

One of the biggest concerns when hiring a solo developer is what happens if that developer takes a full-time job or becomes unavailable.

I address that directly:

1. **Every project is built to be handed off.** Code is clean, documented, and uses standard tools and frameworks. Another developer can pick it up.
2. **You own the code and data.** After final payment, the source code and production credentials are transferred to you.
3. **If my circumstances change, you get advance notice.** I will not disappear. You will receive transition time, handoff documentation, and a plan to keep your system running.
4. **No orphaned code, no unfinished business.** If a project cannot be completed for any reason, I will provide a written status, outstanding tasks, and guidance on how to proceed.

This promise applies to this project and every project I take on.
