# Engagement Letter / Service Agreement

**Client:** Kenning Property Management  
**Contractor:** Dan Spelt  
**Date:** September 10, 2026  
**Project:** Custom Maintenance Request System  
**Quote Reference:** DS-2026-001

---

## 1. Services

Dan Spelt will provide software development services as described in the approved quote and any written change order. The scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees are set out in quote DS-2026-001.

## 2. Fees and Payment

- **Total project fee:** $2,500 CAD
- **Payment schedule:**
  - 40% deposit ($1,000) due on execution of this agreement
  - 40% ($1,000) due on delivery of a working beta
  - 20% ($500) due on final acceptance and handoff
- **Payment terms:** Net 7 days from invoice date. Late payments may delay work.

## 3. Client Responsibilities

The client agrees to:

- Provide timely access to information, stakeholders, and decision-makers.
- Review deliverables and provide feedback within agreed timeframes.
- Provide content, branding, or third-party credentials needed for the project.
- Approve scope changes in writing before work begins.

## 4. Intellectual Property

- The client owns the final source code, design, and data created specifically for this project after final payment is received.
- Dan Spelt retains the right to use general techniques, patterns, and reusable code developed in the course of the work.
- Third-party libraries, frameworks, and services remain under their respective licenses.

## 5. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any business information, customer data, or proprietary processes disclosed during the project, except where required by law or with written permission.

## 6. Limitation of Liability

Dan Spelt's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid by the client for the project. Dan Spelt is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages, including lost profits or business interruption.

## 7. Termination

Either party may terminate the agreement with 14 days' written notice. If the client terminates after work has begun, the client pays for work completed up to that point. If Dan Spelt terminates for reasons outside the client's control, the client receives a proportional refund of any prepaid but unearned fees and a written handoff of work completed to date.

## 8. Continuity and Handoff

Dan Spelt commits to building every project with clean, documented, maintainable code. In the event of unavailability, the client will receive:

- Source code and deployment credentials
- Deployment and environment documentation
- A list of outstanding tasks and next steps
- Reasonable transition time to arrange another developer or support path

## 9. Acceptance

The project is considered accepted when:

- The deliverables match the agreed scope.
- The client has had 14 days to test and raise issues covered by the scope.
- Final payment has been made.

## 10. General

- **Governing Law:** British Columbia, Canada.
- **Changes:** Changes to this agreement must be in writing and signed by both parties.
- **Entire Agreement:** This letter, together with the quote, constitutes the entire agreement.
