# Outreach Target Industries

## Primary Target: Small Property Management Firms

**Why this is the best fit**
- Direct parallel to Community Hive's original use case.
- These firms typically manage communication, maintenance requests, documents, and announcements for multiple buildings.
- Many still rely on email, spreadsheets, paper notices, and WhatsApp groups.
- A 5-20 person firm has enough complexity to need a system, but is small enough to move quickly.

**Ideal firm profile**
- 5-20 employees
- Manages 10-100 residential units or buildings
- Uses email, spreadsheets, or generic tools for tenant/resident communication
- Wants to look more professional and reduce repetitive admin work

## Secondary Targets

1. **HOA / Strata Management Companies**
   - Manage council-resident communication, documents, voting, and maintenance.
   - Similar workflow to Community Hive; case study maps almost directly.

2. **Residential Care / Assisted Living Coordinators**
   - Need private, role-specific dashboards for staff, managers, and families.
   - CareBoard already addresses parts of this workflow.

3. **Small Non-Profit Housing Associations**
   - Often under-resourced and rely on scattered tools.
   - Privacy and audit trail matter.

4. **Facility / Building Maintenance Services**
   - Work order intake, dispatch, status tracking, and client reporting.
   - Can be a narrower, faster initial sale.

## Messaging Angle

Lead with the operational pain, not the technology:

- "Are maintenance requests, announcements, and documents scattered across email and spreadsheets?"
- "What if residents, staff, and managers had one place to see status, read updates, and track issues?"
- Reference Community Hive as a working example without over-explaining the tech.

## Recommended First Move

Start with **small property management firms**. The pitch, quote, and case study material already exist and can be tailored with minimal changes.
