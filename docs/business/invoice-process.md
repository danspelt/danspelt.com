# Invoicing and Payment Process

## Payment Methods

I accept the following payment methods for client work:

1. **Interac e-Transfer** — preferred for Canadian clients, no fees
2. **PayPal** — accepted, client covers any fees
3. **Stripe / Credit Card** — available, standard processing fees apply
4. **Bank transfer (EFT/Wire)** — available for larger engagements

## How Invoicing Works

1. **Deposit invoice** is sent after the engagement letter is signed.
2. **Milestone invoices** are sent at the points described in the quote.
3. **Final invoice** is sent after acceptance and handoff.
4. **Ongoing support** is billed monthly in arrears or in advance, depending on the arrangement.

## Invoice Details

Each invoice will include:

- Dan Spelt contact information and GST/HST number if applicable
- Client company name and contact
- Invoice number and date
- Description of work or milestone
- Amount due
- Payment due date and accepted methods
- Quote reference

## Payment Terms

- **Net 7 days** from invoice date for project work.
- **Net 15 days** for monthly support retainers.
- Late payments may delay further work until the account is brought current.
- Work on a milestone will not begin until the prior milestone payment has cleared.

## Refunds and Disputes

- If a project cannot proceed through no fault of the client, any prepaid but unearned fees will be refunded proportionally.
- Disputes are handled in good faith with written communication and, if needed, a third-party mediator in British Columbia.

## Pricing Guidelines (Internal)

These are internal reference ranges, not public rates. Each quote is tailored to the project.

- Small fix or feature: $500 – $1,500
- Small custom business tool: $2,000 – $5,000
- Larger custom application: $5,000 – $15,000+
- Ongoing support/maintenance: $150 – $500/month
- Discovery conversation: Free
